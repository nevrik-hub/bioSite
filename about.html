About Laura Pavia

Biography

Laura Pavia is a compassionate healthcare professional and vibrant entrepreneur, skillfully balancing a demanding career with diverse creative pursuits and ambitious goals. Residing in Illinois, she currently serves as a dedicated office manager, bringing leadership and organization to her role while actively pursuing a future leadership position in the field.

Laura’s academic achievements are a cornerstone of her journey. She holds a Bachelor of Science degree, a milestone she is deeply proud of. To further her clinical expertise, she also earned her Certified Clinical Medical Assistant (CCMA) certification, demonstrating a commitment to patient care and professional development. Beyond her professional life, Laura is a talented artist and maker with several established hobbies. She is an avid baker and finds joy in painting, creating expressive portraits. Her true entrepreneurial passion lies in candle making; she successfully founded and operates Sweet Cakes Candles LLC, designing her own custom marketing materials for her distinct product line.

Looking ahead, Laura combines her current experience with significant professional aspirations. She is presently pursuing a Master of Science in Healthcare Management Informatics at Colorado Technical University, maintaining an impressive 3.93 GPA with plans to complete her capstone project in February 2027. This advanced degree supports her long-term goal of leading a medical facility focused on geriatric care and mental health administration. Additionally, she holds a unique ambition to one day serve her community in a different capacity as a funeral director. As a devoted mother and fiancée, Laura applies the same dedication and care to her family as she does to her profession and community.
